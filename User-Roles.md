# User Roles

 1. Customer

The customer is the primary user of the e-commerce system.

Customers can:

- Create an account
- Login and manage their profile
- Search and browse products
- View product details
- Add products to the shopping cart
- Place orders
- Make online payments
- Track orders
- Cancel eligible orders
- Request returns and refunds
- Provide product reviews and ratings
 2. Seller

Sellers use the platform to manage their products and sales.

Sellers can:

- Register as a seller
- Add new products
- Update product information
- Manage product prices
- Manage inventory
- View customer orders
- Update order status
- Monitor sales information
3. Administrator

The administrator manages and monitors the overall platform.

Administrators can:

- Manage customer accounts
- Manage seller accounts
- Manage products
- Monitor orders
- Manage complaints
- Monitor transactions
- Generate business reports
- Manage platform settings

4. Delivery Partner

The delivery partner is responsible for delivering customer orders.

Delivery partners can:

- View assigned deliveries
- View delivery information
- Update delivery status
- Confirm successful delivery

5. Payment Service

The payment service handles online transactions.

It can:

- Process customer payments
- Verify payment status
- Provide transaction confirmation
- Handle payment failures
- Support refund processing

Summary

Each user role has specific responsibilities. Together, these roles
allow the e-commerce system to manage the complete business process
from product listing to final delivery.
