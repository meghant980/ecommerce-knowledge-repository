# Business Overview
 Introduction

An e-commerce business system is a digital platform that allows
businesses to sell products and services to customers through the
internet.

The system connects customers, sellers, administrators, payment
services, inventory management, and delivery operations.

Business Objectives

The main objectives of an e-commerce system are:

- Provide customers with convenient online shopping.
- Allow sellers to manage products and inventory.
- Process customer orders efficiently.
- Provide secure online payment facilities.
- Track orders from purchase to delivery.
- Support returns and refunds.
- Maintain customer and transaction information.

Products and Services

The platform may provide different categories of products such as:

- Electronics
- Clothing
- Home appliances
- Books
- Grocery products
- Personal care products

## Revenue Model

An e-commerce business can generate revenue through:

- Product sales
- Seller commissions
- Delivery charges
- Advertising and promoted product listings
- Subscription or membership services

## Key Business Operations

The major operations include:

1. Customer registration
2. Product listing
3. Product search
4. Cart management
5. Order placement
6. Payment processing
7. Inventory management
8. Order fulfillment
9. Delivery
10. Returns and refunds

Business Stakeholders

The major stakeholders are:

- Customers
- Sellers
- Administrators
- Delivery partners
- Payment service providers
- Business management
Conclusion

An e-commerce business system provides an integrated platform for
online buying and selling. It coordinates multiple business
activities and helps businesses manage customers, products, orders,
payments, inventory, and deliveries efficiently.
