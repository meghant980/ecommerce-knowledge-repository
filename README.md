E-Commerce Business System – Knowledge Repository

 1. Introduction

This repository contains structured knowledge about a real-world
E-Commerce Business System.

It documents the major business processes, users, requirements,
system architecture, database design, APIs, security, and common
questions related to an e-commerce platform.

2. Business Overview

An e-commerce system allows customers to browse products, search for
items, add products to a shopping cart, place orders, make payments,
and track deliveries.

The system also supports sellers and administrators in managing
products, inventory, orders, customers, and business operations.

and easy-to-understand source of knowledge about an e-commerce

 7. Conclusion

An e-commerce system integrates customers, sellers, administrators,
payment services, inventory, and delivery operations into a single
business platform.
