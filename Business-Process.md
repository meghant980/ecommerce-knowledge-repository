# Business Process

## Overview

The e-commerce business process describes the complete journey of a
customer from searching for a product to receiving the order.

## Step 1: User Registration and Login

The customer creates an account by providing the required information.
After registration, the customer can log in to access the platform.

## Step 2: Product Search

The customer searches for a required product using keywords,
categories, filters, or sorting options.

## Step 3: Product Selection

The customer views product information such as:

- Product name
- Description
- Price
- Availability
- Ratings and reviews

The customer selects the required product.

## Step 4: Add to Cart

The selected product is added to the shopping cart.

The customer can:

- Change the quantity
- Remove products
- Add more products
- View the total amount

## Step 5: Checkout

The customer proceeds to checkout and provides:

- Delivery address
- Contact information
- Preferred delivery option

The system calculates the final order amount.

## Step 6: Payment

The customer selects an available payment method and completes the
payment through the payment service.

The system verifies the payment status.

If the payment is successful, the order is confirmed.

## Step 7: Order Processing

The seller receives the order and prepares the product for shipment.

The inventory is updated according to the purchased quantity.

## Step 8: Shipping

The prepared order is handed over to the delivery partner.

The order status is updated to indicate that it has been shipped.

## Step 9: Delivery

The delivery partner delivers the order to the customer's address.

After successful delivery, the system updates the order status to
"Delivered".

## Step 10: Return and Refund

If the product is eligible for return, the customer can submit a return
request.

After the return is approved and processed, the eligible refund is
initiated.

## Conclusion

The e-commerce business process connects customers, sellers,
inventory, payment services, and delivery partners to complete an
online purchase efficiently.
